# UniKorn Context Map

## Contexts

- [Course Planning](./CONTEXT.md) — builds, saves, and shares semester timetables.
- [MeetCampus](./docs/meetcampus/CONTEXT.md) — lets persistent campus residents live, form relationships, and create owner-approved real-world icebreakers.

## Relationships

- **UniKorn Identity → MeetCampus**: an authenticated UniKorn user may become the owner of one resident when the feature access policy allows it.
- **MeetCampus → UniKorn Identity**: MeetCampus keeps real-user identity outside resident-to-resident interaction until an owner-approved real-world bridge permits disclosure.
